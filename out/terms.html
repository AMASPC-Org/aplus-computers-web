Terms of Service

Effective Date: October 20, 2026

1. Electronics Recycling & Equipment Surrender

As an authorized Washington State E-Cycle Partner, A+ Computers Olympia accepts qualifying electronic waste—including computers, laptops, monitors, cell phones, tablets, printers, and accessories—during posted drop-off hours (Tuesday – Friday, 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM). By dropping off equipment for recycling, you confirm that you are the rightful owner (or authorized agent) of the items and permanently transfer ownership of the surrendered hardware to A+ Computers Olympia upon drop-off.

2. Data Destruction & Customer Backup Responsibility

All storage media dropped off for e-recycling is permanently sanitized or physically destroyed. Once an item is surrendered for recycling, data recovery is impossible. Customers are solely responsible for backing up any personal files, photos, or records prior to dropping off devices.

3. Unaccepted & Hazardous Materials

Please do not leave electronics outside our building when the shop is closed (Saturday – Monday). We reserve the right to decline appliances, leaking batteries, bio-contaminated equipment, or non-electronic household hazardous waste.

4. Computer Repair & Refurbished Sales

All in-shop computer diagnostics, hardware repairs, upgrades, and refurbished desktop or laptop purchases are governed by the specific service authorization and warranty terms provided on your physical in-store receipt at 2724 Pacific Ave SE, Olympia, WA 98501.
